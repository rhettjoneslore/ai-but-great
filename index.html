Memo

Perfect AI writing a ginormous opportunity we are at the cusp of.

Frontier AI models and open-source models are JUST starting to get good enough to build on and fine-tune on, respectively. With utmost-quality datasets, and ML work / fine-tuning, it’s within possibility to make a product that:

  1. Writes exactly like a human (undetectable as AI writing – this wasn't even possible in August!)
  2. Stays as intelligent as the frontier model itself (current AI humanizers / editors have major intelligence issues)
  3. Writes only at a top 1% level (e.g. purely training it on small, best-of-the-best cold outbound email datasets from high-end companies – not general cold outbound email datasets)

The first product:

The core consumer product will be a generalist ChatGPT-style chatbot that doesn't sound like AI.

Aiding this core product will be, firstly, a voice list (e.g. from 100+ voice options, choose a “website copy voice” trained on ~30k words of top 1% websites), and secondly, a two-click-install context agent (an agent that pulls full context on you / your business from your computer, in a non-security-breaching way, into an optionally constantly updated doc that you and the AI can both read from, so you can ask it to write, for example, website copy, and it know the exact details of what you’re working on).

Why consumer: First, we’ll make a lot of money really fast. Mainly, we’ll go viral – having the "AI that sounds human" brand makes B2B sales cook. As a first mover, we'll undoubtedly win distribution with an ecom-style campaign for consumer and a high-ticket-sales-style campaign for B2B.

The B2B opportunity:

Let’s use Cased, my previous company, as an example:

Stage 1: We write really well. At $2k/mo, the employees in the Philippines use our product to write all of their emails, but it’s not automated (e.g. they manually update the inventory count in Shopify themselves, they check photos that customers send of a product they received, etc.). This is how most current AI email customer service companies work – and we can pretty easily have the best one in a couple months!

Stage 2: At $20k/mo, we just do the email customer service. The whole service is entirely done-for-you (with a human-in-the-loop on the backend). The company never thinks about it again + we outperform their best humans.

How the B2B product works (stage 1):

  1. Get the writing style from a small, top-quality dataset. Examples: 1) ~10k words of a researcher’s writing, or 2) a top-performing brand’s Meta ad scripts, or 3) quotes from a produce distributor.
  2. Get full context on the project. Examples: 1) All docs of the research project you’re working on, or 2) the brand's website and past Meta ads, or 3) the produce distributor’s entire quote history and software.
  3. Have an AI write exactly like the dataset (human-sounding), and accurately because it has the context. Examples: 1) a report that sounds fully like you wrote it, or 2) a Meta ad script that performs as well as those from the brand's best copywriter, or 3) a highly personalized, human-sounding quote that hits all points.
